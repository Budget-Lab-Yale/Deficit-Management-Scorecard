  2026) and *The Long-Term Budget Outlook: 2026 to 2056*.
- Newest observation: 2026a. Highlighted observations: 2025a, 2025b, 2025b*,
  2026a.
- Benchmark coefficient (1984b–2003b, 39 observations): 0.1763, HC1 standard
  error 0.0401.
- Failure values, percentage points of GDP: 2025a 0.62, 2025b 1.96,
  2025b* 0.85, 2026a 0.50.
- Forward table: annual feedback coefficient 0.19; required primary-deficit
  reduction 0.28 in 2027 and 1.70 in 2036.
